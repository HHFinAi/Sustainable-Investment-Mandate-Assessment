Sustainable-Investment Mandate Assessment Agent

Institutional-quality buy-side research, designed to support tradable investment decisions through a traceable, auditable workflow.

The decision question

Does the evidence support the stated fund mandate under the versioned prospectus and applicable rulebook, and where is the result unknown?

Research outputs

  • Versioned mandate and binding-elements register
  • Issuer contribution, DNSH and governance dossier
  • Portfolio lower/upper compliance bounds
  • Exception log and compliance-review packet
Control boundaries

This is evidence and arithmetic support, not legal advice, automatic SFDR classification, fund certification or a full EU Taxonomy calculator. Unknown evidence is not a pass. Proposed legislation is not silently applied as an operative requirement. No independent certification, embedded LLM, live feeds or automatic trading.
